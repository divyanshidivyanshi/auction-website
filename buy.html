FAQs
How to bid in BidWave auctions?

You can bid in BidWave auctions in a few easy steps. All you need to do is to create an account on our website and complete registration and verification formalities to get started.

How soon before the auctions does a prospective bid?

If you are not previously registered to bid with us, you are required to start the registration process at least 4 (four) Business Days before the auction date..

What is the value of the opening bid?

The value of the opening bid shall be determined as follows:
a) The opening bid shall be 10% lower than the value of the lower estimate.
b) In case of a 'No Reserve' auction, the opening bid shall be at INR 20,000. (A ‘No Reserve’ auction is one in which no ‘Reserve Price’ has been set.).

How to I pay for my purchase?

Once your bidding has been successful, you will receive an email with the invoice stating the final purchase price payable, including the hammer price, the margin, and other costs to be paid by the buyer. All sales in India will attract GST ranging from 3% to 28% according to different categories. You will be required to complete the payment within seven business days from the receipt of the invoice. Any delay in payment will be liable for a 2% interest charged per month..
